# Q-003 — Does a brownout preserve the RTC domain?

gimbal-bench · next · gate none

## What this is

**The brownout case**, unknown in both directions. A brownout may or may not preserve the RTC domain, and nothing here has produced one. The M5 ring distinguishes "the fixture rebooted itself" from "somebody cut the power" using three agreeing signals — reset reason, `lost` flag, and whether the flash mirror had to be restored. A brownout is the case that table has no row for, and a ceiling fixture on household mains will meet one.
